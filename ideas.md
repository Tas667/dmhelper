## Deep Dives into Scrapped Concepts

### 1. ~~Modular tabletop terrain generator for quick dungeon builds~~ (Scrapped Exploration)
- **Concept Snapshot:** Procedural toolkit that assembles modular dungeon rooms and corridors for tabletop campaigns before being abandoned.
- **User Interaction:** Game masters would drag and drop terrain pieces into a map canvas with snapping guides.
- **Friction Point:** Requires a vast asset library to feel varied; curating enough quality modules proved overwhelming.
- **Randomness Factor:** Medium; random seed options would shuffle layouts but still rely on prebuilt tiles.
- **Usefulness Depth:** High potential for prep speed, yet scrapped due to resource demands exceeding payoff.

### 2. ~~AI-assisted NPC dialogue writer with personality presets~~ (Scrapped Exploration)
- **Concept Snapshot:** Dialogue generator that tailored NPC speech to archetypes before the idea was shelved.
- **User Interaction:** Users would select a personality preset, enter prompts, and receive suggested dialogue snippets.
- **Friction Point:** Fine-tuning tone consistency across scenes introduced constant editing headaches.
- **Randomness Factor:** High; stochastic language models could produce unexpected phrasing needing oversight.
- **Usefulness Depth:** Considerable, but the supervision load negated the promised efficiency, so it was scrapped.

### 3. ~~Dynamic encounter balancer based on party composition~~ (Scrapped Exploration)

- **Randomness Factor:** Low; deterministic calculations meant predictable outputs with minimal surprise.
- **Usefulness Depth:** Solid theoretical value, yet upkeep friction outpaced benefits and led to scrapping.
